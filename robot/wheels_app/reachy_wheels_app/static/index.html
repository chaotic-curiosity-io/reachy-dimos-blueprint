Rotating in place is what mecanum wheels are worst
          at: all four rollers scrub sideways at once, so the lightest
          corner slips first and the pivot walks. Try the presets, watch
          which one actually spins about its own centre, and note the mix
          line — that is what gets baked into the firmware's rotate
          primitives.

Talk to Reachy: “what’s around you?”, “look left”,
        “turn to face me”, “come here”, “follow the cat”, “stop”. It moves in
        tiers — glances with its head, rotates its body, and only rolls the
        wheels to actually travel. Voice drives are capped at 4 s per
        command.
